# OPDS Reader

[Русский](README.md) · [Download](https://github.com/Grimsel1/opds-reader/releases/latest) · [Privacy Policy](https://grimsel1.github.io/opds-reader-privacy/privacy-en.html)

An Android reader with a personal library, adjustable page layout and text-to-speech. Open your local files, connect your own OPDS catalogs and follow new releases from favorite authors and series.

**Android 8.0+ · English and Russian · Free, with no built-in ads or purchases**

## Features

- EPUB, FB2, PDF, DjVu, TXT, HTML, MOBI/AZW/AZW3, DOCX, RTF and CBZ/CBR comics.
- Covers, authors, series, collections, reading status and scanning of user-selected folders.
- User-added OPDS catalogs, downloads and author/series subscriptions. No online catalogs are preinstalled.
- Fonts, themes, margins, spacing, paged or scrolling reading and configurable controls.
- Bookmarks, highlights, notes, library search and a shared knowledge center.
- PDF page fitting, panning, OCR and supported form filling.
- Android voices and local neural speech after an optional model download, with a sleep timer and pronunciation rules.
- Parallel reading, translation, audiobooks and text/audio links.
- Study cards, review scheduling and Anki export.
- Backups and optional Mail.ru Cloud synchronization.

Core reading works offline. Optional services and models may require a network connection, a separate account or a personal API key; external providers set their own prices. DRM-protected books are not supported. Rendering depends on the document structure.

## Install

Open the [latest release](https://github.com/Grimsel1/opds-reader/releases/latest), download **OPDS-Reader-0.5.41.apk**, open the file and follow the Android prompts. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is provided beside the APK for download integrity checks.
